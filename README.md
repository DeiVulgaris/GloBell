# GloBell

## Global Bell

A location-based communication platform connecting people, services, businesses, requests, and opportunities in space and time.

GloBell does not require you to know **who** to contact.

You define **where** and **what you need**.

```text
LOCATION
   ↓
REQUEST / OFFER
   ↓
CATEGORY + TAG
   ↓
MATCHING
   ↓
PEOPLE / BUSINESS / SERVICE
   ↓
CONTACT
```

## Core Idea

A user can search for people or opportunities in any location.

The location does not have to be the user's current location.

```text
WHERE + WHAT
```

Examples:

* Find an available mechanic within 5 km.
* Find a photographer at an event in another country.
* Find windsurfers in a resort town.
* Find a restaurant in New York.
* Offer a working refrigerator to people nearby.
* Ask for help when a car breaks down.
* Publish a short-lived local announcement.

## People

Users may define:

* skills
* interests
* services
* availability
* categories
* communication preferences

A user may be visible to the network only while actively participating.

Contact information is not automatically exposed.

A contact request requires the other user to accept it.

Each acceptance applies to a specific contact, rather than creating permanent access.

## Profiles

Users may maintain personal profiles describing who they are and what they can offer.

Profiles may be:

* public
* partially visible
* available only to the matching system

Users control the amount of information they expose.

## Reputation

Participation can build a reputation within the network.

Reputation may influence access to higher-trust functions and help users evaluate potential contacts.

Trust is intended to become an important part of the platform.

## Businesses

Businesses can exist as persistent location-based objects.

A business may provide:

* services
* opening hours
* contact information
* reputation
* location
* relevant categories and tags

## Temporary Announcements

GloBell can support short-lived location-based announcements.

An announcement may exist for a limited period, such as 24 hours.

Users may optionally enable automatic renewal.

## SOS and High-Trust Requests

The platform may support controlled high-risk requests such as:

* SOS
* lost persons
* danger alerts
* urgent local assistance

These functions require stronger trust and reputation controls.

## AI Search

Users do not have to know the correct category or tag.

They can describe what they need naturally.

AI can transform the request into searchable parameters and match it against relevant profiles.

```text
Natural language
      ↓
AI interpretation
      ↓
LOCATION + CATEGORY + TAG + AVAILABILITY
      ↓
MATCHING
```

AI can also help resolve ambiguous requests and improve search through interaction feedback.

## Self-Employment

GloBell can provide a low-barrier way for individuals to offer skills and services locally.

Examples:

* manicure
* appliance repair
* photography
* programming
* vehicle repair
* translation
* tutoring

A person does not have to operate a large business to become discoverable.

## Communities and Interests

Users may form persistent interest categories such as:

* truck drivers
* doctors
* hang gliders
* windsurfers
* musicians
* hobby groups

A location-based filter can show relevant people and businesses in a selected area.

## Map First

The map is the primary interface.

The user can:

1. choose a location;
2. define a radius graphically;
3. select or describe what they need;
4. see relevant people, businesses, services, announcements, or events.

The user's current location is only one possible starting point.

Any point on the map can become a search location.

## Vision

GloBell is intended to become a platform where **people, capabilities, needs, businesses, and information are connected through location**.

The initial concept is deliberately small.

The architecture should allow additional services to be added without changing the underlying principle.

> **First the place. Then everything connected to it.**
